third-party projects, their licenses, and where to obtain full license texts.

pyqtgraph: MIT, https://github.com/pyqtgraph/pyqtgraph
PyOpenGL: BSD-style, https://pyopengl.sourceforge.net/
NumPy: BSD-3-Clause, https://numpy.org/about/
Astropy / astropy-iers-data / PyERFA: BSD-3-Clause, https://www.astropy.org/
sgp4: MIT, https://pypi.org/project/sgp4/
Requests: Apache-2.0, https://requests.readthedocs.io/
platformdirs: MIT, https://github.com/tox-dev/platformdirs

For exact package versions see requirements.txt.
